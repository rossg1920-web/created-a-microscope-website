About VividScope

Precision optics and expert service for research, education, and industry since 2010.

Our Story

Clarity Is Everything

We started with a simple belief: every microscope should deliver reliable, high-quality performance — and when it doesn’t, expert help should be easy to find.

VividScope was founded by optical technicians and educators who were frustrated by long repair queues, opaque pricing, and instruments that didn’t match their promised performance. We set out to build a company that combines carefully selected microscopes with genuinely expert service.

Today we supply schools, universities, research labs, quality-control departments, and hobbyists across North America. Our in-house technicians are trained on major platforms from Olympus, Nikon, Zeiss, Leica, and others, and we maintain a deep inventory of parts so most repairs move quickly.

Whether you need a classroom set of compound microscopes, a research-grade fluorescence system, or a careful restoration of a trusted instrument, we’re here to help you see more clearly.
