Services

What We Fix

Comprehensive service for optical, mechanical, and electronic systems.

🔍

Optical Services

Objective and eyepiece cleaning, antireflective coating care, prism alignment, condenser repair, and full optical path calibration.

⚙️

Mechanical Repairs

Focus drive rebuilds, stage repair/replacement, nosepiece turret service, stand restoration, and lubrication of all moving parts.

💡

Illumination & Electronics

LED conversions, power supply repair, lamp house service, camera port electronics, and control panel diagnostics.

📐

Calibration

NIST-traceable magnification and stage calibration. Certificates suitable for ISO and GLP documentation.

🔄

Full Restoration

Complete overhaul of vintage or heavily used instruments — optics, mechanics, cosmetics, and performance testing.

📅

Maintenance Plans

Annual or semi-annual preventive maintenance contracts for labs and schools. Priority scheduling and discounted parts.
